# MyGolfSpy (MGS) Most Wanted Iron Testing: Forgiveness and Accuracy Rankings, Game-Improvement (GI) and Super Game-Improvement (SGI), 2017-2026

> **Method caveat (read first):** This environment's egress policy blocked direct fetches of mygolfspy.com, forum.mygolfspy.com and web.archive.org (HTTP 403 on CONNECT). Every data point below comes from search-engine extracts of MGS's own pages, not from reading the full pages or results tables. The URLs are the original MGS pages. Numbers that appeared verbatim in the extracts are reported as-is. Where extracts disagreed or looked garbled, I flag it. Treat ranks below the top 3 and exact sub-scores as "verify on page."

## Q1. Year-by-year: which GI / SGI irons ranked top for forgiveness and accuracy (with numbers)?

### Takeaway
MGS's scoring has changed over time: 2017-2021 it used Strokes Gained/TRUERank, 2022-2023 a 100-point score split into accuracy/forgiveness/distance, and 2024-2026 x/10 sub-scores, where accuracy is based on Strokes Gained and forgiveness on consistency (ball-speed, carry and spin deltas plus dispersion/shot area). The most recent forgiveness leaders are the **Srixon ZXiR (GI 2026, 9.2)** and **Cobra KING MAX (SGI 2026, 9.3)**. The most recent accuracy leaders are the **Takomo Iron 101 MKII (GI 2026, 9.5)** and **Tour Edge Hot Launch Max D (SGI 2026)**. In the SGI class, the **Tommy Armour 845+** led forgiveness in both 2024 and 2025.

### Cited Findings

**How MGS scores forgiveness and accuracy (methodology)**
- 2024-2026: MGS treats forgiveness as consistency, measured by deltas in ball speed, carry distance and backspin, together with overall dispersion. — [MGS, Most Forgiving Irons of 2026](https://mygolfspy.com/news-opinion/best-irons-for-forgiveness-what-the-2026-data-shows/)
- Accuracy is measured with Strokes Gained. In GI testing, MGS describes it as how accurate an iron is "on or near the green" in its test conditions, using a 5-iron, 7-iron and pitching wedge. — [MGS, Top 3 Most Accurate GI Irons](https://mygolfspy.com/buyers-guides/all/top-3-most-accurate-game-improvement-irons/); [MGS, Srixon ZX4 MK II review](https://mygolfspy.com/reviews/irons/srixon-zx4-mk-ii-irons-review/)
- In 2024 SGI, "Accuracy is measured mainly by Strokes Gained." — [MGS, Best SGI Irons of 2024](https://mygolfspy.com/buyers-guides/irons/best-super-game-improvement-irons-of-2024/)
- In 2022, MGS used a 100-point scoring system split into three categories: accuracy, forgiveness and distance. — [MGS, Best SGI Irons 2022](https://mygolfspy.com/buyers-guides/irons/2022-most-wanted-super-game-improvement-iron/)
- In 2018 GI, MGS tested 17 models over 9,000+ shots. The winner was the club that finished in the statistically significant top group, based on Strokes Gained, for the highest percentage of testers ("TRUERank"). The 2018 SGI test used 5,049 shots and 20 testers. — [MGS, 2018 Most Wanted GI Iron](https://mygolfspy.com/buyers-guides/irons/2018-most-wanted-game-improvement-iron/); [MGS, 2018 Most Wanted SGI Iron](https://mygolfspy.com/buyers-guides/irons/2018-most-wanted-super-game-improvement-iron/)
- In 2019, forgiveness was reported as 7-iron shot area in square yards (yds², smaller = tighter). — [MGS, 2019 Most Wanted GI Iron](https://mygolfspy.com/buyers-guides/irons/2019-most-wanted-game-improvement-iron/)

### Inferences
- For a golfer who doesn't need distance, the 2026 data suggest the Srixon ZXiR (GI forgiveness 1st) and Takomo Iron 101 MKII (GI accuracy 1st at 9.5, forgiveness 2nd at 9.1) are the strongest GI forgiveness-plus-accuracy combinations. Takomo is the only 2026 GI model that ranks top 2 in both. In 2026 SGI, the Tour Edge Hot Launch Max D (forgiveness 9.1, accuracy 1st) is the best two-way performer. The KING MAX is the most forgiving but explicitly less precise.
- Several forgiveness and accuracy leaders were *short* irons, for example the Orka CTi SF, Fourteen TB-5 and Takomo. This tradeoff fits a user who doesn't need distance.
- The 0-10 scores from 2024-2026 are not directly comparable with the 100-point scores from 2022-2023 or the Strokes-Gained/TRUERank results from 2017-2021.

### Gaps
- Full per-iron tables for each year, including shot area (yds²), carry SD and offline values, were not accessible because direct fetches were blocked. Only the numbers quoted in search extracts are reported.
- 2017, 2018 GI, 2020 GI and 2022 GI forgiveness and accuracy leaders were not extracted.
- The 2025 GI forgiveness 3rd place is unclear (Orka CTi SF vs Sub 70 699 v3), and the 699 v3's exact forgiveness score is unconfirmed.
- The 2026 Cobra KING MAX shot area and carry delta values were not found.

## Q2. Which models repeatedly appear at the top of forgiveness/accuracy sub-rankings across years?

### Takeaway
The repeat performers in MGS data are the **Mizuno JPX Hot Metal / Hot Metal HL** line, the **Tommy Armour 845+**, **Tour Edge Hot Launch / Exotics**, **Cobra F-Max / T-Rail / KING MAX** and, since 2023, **Srixon ZX4 Mk II / ZXi4 / ZXiR**. Ping G-series GI irons did not lead MGS forgiveness or accuracy sub-rankings in the data found, and the Ping G740 was last in 2026 SGI forgiveness.
